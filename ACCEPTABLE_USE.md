# Acceptable Use

Realtime Agent Lab is intended for consent-based development, testing, accessibility, meeting assistance, customer support, and research.

## Required

- Obtain informed consent before recording or analyzing another person's audio, screen, or communications.
- Follow local law, organizational policy, and platform rules.
- Protect API keys, recordings, transcripts, and personal information.
- Make the presence and role of an AI assistant clear when disclosure is required.

## Prohibited project use

- Secret assistance in exams, certifications, or interviews where outside assistance is prohibited.
- Impersonation or misrepresentation of a person's abilities.
- Covert surveillance.
- Collection of personal data without a lawful basis.
- Circumventing security, access controls, or proctoring systems.

Maintainers may reject contributions that primarily enable these uses.
